Meet
Mitthu.

A small companion for your chores and weekends.

Talk it through like you would with a friend. Mitthu is being built to turn what you agree on into reminders and plans you can see, edit and trust — even when the AI is offline.

Why Mitthu?

A friendly face,
with a good memory for chores.

Most assistants are good at talking and forgetful about doing. Mitthu is built the other way round: the conversation is the easy part, and every reminder it suggests becomes a real record on your phone once you approve it.

You approve each one. You can open it, change it, snooze it or delete it with ordinary forms. Mitthu never claims a reminder is set unless Android has actually scheduled it.

How it works

From a chat to a
real reminder.

Text conversation works in the current build, which is not released yet: tap the face, then type. Nothing is saved or changed until you approve it on a card. So far it has been tried by hand on two phones, and the newest parts (weekend plans and memory) on one.

  1. 1

    You say it

    “Remind me to water the plants every Sunday morning.” Tap the face and type it. Voice comes later.

  2. 2

    Mitthu drafts it

    Mitthu replies in one short sentence and, when you mention a chore, shows a card. A new chore's card offers Add chore, Edit first or Not now.

  3. 3

    You approve, it's saved

    Tap Add chore and it is saved on your device, with its true status: scheduled, needs permission, or failed. If the save fails, Mitthu says so.

What it does

Built now,
and what's next.

Chores & reminders

One-time, daily and weekly chores with a visible next reminder, snooze and Done.

Text conversation

Tap the face and type. Mitthu answers briefly and offers a card; nothing is saved until you approve it. You can also ask it to change a chore's time, days, repeat or title, or to archive it. Every change shows before and after and needs your approval. Deleting stays in the chore list. “Weekend” follows your region.

Your AI, your key

Choose Gemini (the default), OpenAI, NVIDIA or OpenRouter in Settings. Your API key is stored encrypted on the phone and checked with the provider before it is saved. Mitthu sends your messages, your active chores' titles and schedules (not notes) and the memory facts you've switched on to the provider you choose.

History stays on your phone

Mitthu keeps your last 7 days of conversation on your phone only, with a Clear button. Earlier conversations aren't backed up and aren't sent to the AI.

Export & import your data

Save your chores, which ones you have done, your open reminders, your weekend plans and your saved memory to a file you choose, and import it on another phone. Importing replaces everything on that phone, after two confirmations; Mitthu keeps a safety copy of what was there first, so you can undo the last import. A damaged or newer file changes nothing. The file isn't encrypted, so anyone who has it can read your chores, plans and memory: keep it somewhere private. It never contains your API keys or chat history.

Works without the AI

With no AI or key, chores, weekend plans and memory still work through the forms.

Weekend plans

Pick your dates, then add, edit, remove and reorder what you'd like to do. Plans are saved on your phone and work offline. Ask Mitthu in chat to plan a weekend and it shows a draft: nothing is saved until you tap Save plan, and if you have edited a plan by hand you choose Keep my edits or Replace with draft.

Memory

Mitthu saves a fact only when you approve its suggestion in chat or type one yourself. Each fact has a “Use in replies” switch: every fact switched on is sent to your AI provider with each message, up to 50, and one switched off is never sent. You can edit or delete any fact. Deleting a fact on your phone stops it being used right away, but can't recall what was already sent to the provider. Mitthu never collects memory in the background.

Voice, later

Planned. Push-to-talk and spoken replies, with typing always available.

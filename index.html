Why Mitthu?

A friendly face,
with a good memory for chores.

Most assistants are good at talking and forgetful about doing. Mitthu is built the other way round: the conversation is the easy part, and every reminder or plan it suggests becomes a real record on your phone.

You approve each one. You can open it, change it, snooze it or delete it with ordinary forms. Mitthu never claims a reminder is set unless Android has actually scheduled it.

How it will work

From a chat to a
real reminder.

Conversation is in development. Today you create chores with a form, and their reminders already work this way.

  1. 1

    You say it

    “Remind me to water the plants every Sunday morning.” Type it, or later, hold to talk.

  2. 2

    Mitthu drafts it

    A draft chore appears with a time, timezone and repeat rule. Anything unclear stays a draft until you fix it.

  3. 3

    You approve, it's scheduled

    The chore is saved on your device and you see its true status: scheduled, needs permission, or failed.

What's planned

Everything it
will be.

Chores & reminders

One-time, daily and weekly chores with a visible next reminder, snooze and completion history.

Weekend planner

Pick your dates and free time. Get an editable, dated plan — your manual edits are never overwritten.

Memory you control

Mitthu remembers only what you choose to save. See it, edit it, delete it.

Text first, voice optional

Push-to-talk and spoken replies, with typing always available as a fallback.

Works without the AI

Chores and plans live in a local database and stay editable offline.

Phone & tablet

On a tablet, Mitthu can sit beside your task. Large text and reduced motion are supported.

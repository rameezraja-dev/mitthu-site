Why Mitthu?

A friendly face,
with a good memory for chores.

Most assistants are good at talking and forgetful about doing. Mitthu is built the other way round: the conversation is the easy part, and every reminder or plan it suggests becomes a real record on your phone.

You approve each one. You can open it, change it, snooze it or delete it with ordinary forms. Mitthu never claims a reminder is set unless Android has actually scheduled it.

How it works

From a chat to a
real reminder.

Text conversation works in the current build, which is not released yet: tap the face, then type. Nothing is saved until you tap Add chore. So far it has been tried on one phone.

  1. 1

    You say it

    “Remind me to water the plants every Sunday morning.” Tap the face and type it. Voice comes later.

  2. 2

    Mitthu drafts it

    Mitthu replies with one short question and, when you mention a chore, a card with three choices: Add chore, Edit first or Not now.

  3. 3

    You approve, it's saved

    Tap Add chore and it is saved on your device, with its true status: scheduled, needs permission, or failed. If the save fails, Mitthu says so.

Personality

It has a few
things to say.

  • “Bins go out tonight.”
  • “Laundry's at six. Want to push it to seven?”
  • “Saturday looks free after lunch.”
  • “You said you liked that walk by the lake.”

Sample lines for tone, not real replies. Memory and weekend plans are not built yet. Real replies depend on the AI provider and model you pick, using your own API key.

What it does

Built now,
and what's next.

Chores & reminders

One-time, daily and weekly chores with a visible next reminder, snooze and Done.

Text conversation

Tap the face and type. Mitthu answers briefly and offers a card; nothing is saved until you tap Add chore.

Your AI, your key

Choose Gemini (the default), OpenAI, NVIDIA or OpenRouter in Settings. Your API key is stored encrypted on the phone and checked with the provider before it is saved.

Works without the AI

With no AI or key, chores still work through the forms.

Weekend plans & memory

Planned. Pick your dates and free time for an editable plan, and let Mitthu remember only what you choose to save.

Voice, later

Planned. Push-to-talk and spoken replies, with typing always available.
